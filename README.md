# fmtv-trailer

A one-page YouTube player for [Free My TV](https://github.com/malmtuna/free-my-tv)'s trailers on Samsung TVs.

YouTube refuses to embed videos in pages that don't say which website they're on, and an app installed on a TV has no
website. Free My TV shows this page instead, at https://malmtuna.github.io/fmtv-trailer/?v=VIDEO_ID, which plays the
video and tells Free My TV when it's ready, playing, paused, ended or refused.

It stores nothing and collects nothing: it only plays the video it's given.
